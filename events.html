Formats

Three kinds of night.

The Showcase

Member companies and invited builders demo what they've shipped this quarter. Real products, live, with the numbers behind them where founders are willing to share.

Quarterly · 90 minutes

Speaker evening

One member, one topic, told properly — first ten customers, a failed hire, what a term sheet actually cost. Followed by open questions.

Monthly · 60 minutes

Builders' meetup

No stage. For students, first-time builders and anyone deciding whether to start. Members come to answer questions, not to present.

Speaking

What speakers are asked to do.

  • Bring one specific thing you learned the hard way, with the detail intact.
  • Use real numbers wherever you're allowed to.
  • No product pitch, no hiring pitch, no fundraising pitch.
  • Stay for the questions — that's the part the room came for.
