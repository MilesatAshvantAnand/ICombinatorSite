For members

Everything the circle gives you.

This page is for companies inside the I Combinator inner circle. If you're not yet a member, see how to apply instead.

Benefits

What's available.

Peer sessions

Closed, off-the-record sessions with other member founders. Agenda is whatever the room is actually stuck on that month.

Shared resources

Legal and hiring templates, tooling recommendations, and negotiated rates as members bring them into the circle.

Introductions

Warm routes to customers, engineers and investors — made directly by a member who will vouch for you.

A public platform

Speaking slots at showcases and speaker evenings, under the I Combinator name rather than as a solo company pitch.

National reach

Standing invitations to events and meetups across Dublin, Cork, Galway and Limerick.

The directory

Direct contact with every other member company — the actual reason the circle is worth being small.

Access

There's no login yet — just email us.

I Combinator doesn't have a members' portal in place yet. Until it does, access to anything on this page — the resource library, an introduction, a speaking slot, the next peer session — goes through a direct email. Say which company you're with and what you need; a member of the circle will follow up.
