Apply — inner circle

Read by a member. Answered either way.

There is no automated screen and no scoring form. A member of the circle reads every application and replies within ten working days, including when the answer is no.

Before you start

What we're actually looking for.

  • Something live. Users, revenue, a product people can use, or a company already trading. Ideas on their own aren't enough yet.
  • Early in your career. The circle is built for young founders — students, dropouts and first-timers are all in scope.
  • An Irish connection. Building in Ireland, or building from Ireland for a market elsewhere.
  • Willingness to give back. Members answer each other's questions honestly. That obligation is the membership fee.

Not a fit if

You're looking for a channel to sell to other founders, you want the introductions without answering anyone else's questions, or there's nothing built yet. None of those are character judgements — they're just not what this room is for. The public events are open to everyone regardless.

The process

What happens after you send it.

A member reads it

Not a form-filter. Someone who has built something reads what you wrote, in full.

A conversation

If it's a possible fit, a half-hour call — about what you're building and what you'd want from the circle.

A decision

Within ten working days of the call. You get a real answer, not silence.

An introduction

If it's a yes, you're introduced to the circle directly and invited to the next closed session.

The circle is kept deliberately small. We would rather take one member a month and have the room stay useful than fill it and lose the reason it works.
