How it works

Two layers, one community.

Most communities pick one: a private club nobody can see into, or an open meetup with no substance behind it. We run both, deliberately, and keep them connected.

Layer one — closed

The inner circle

A vetted group of young Irish founders already building real companies. Members trade the things founders normally guess at: pricing, hiring, fundraising terms, suppliers, tooling, and introductions.

  • Peer sessions — closed, off the record
  • Shared vendor rates, tooling and templates
  • Warm introductions across the network
  • Direct lines to members who've solved it already

Layer two — public

The public programme

Open showcases, speaker nights and meetups anyone can attend. Inner-circle members speak here on behalf of I Combinator — not as a pitch for their own company.

  • Showcases — what's being built in Ireland now
  • Speaker evenings, free to attend
  • Meetups for first-time and student builders
  • Open to anyone curious, not just members

The principle

Members speak for the community, not their cap table.

The rule that makes the public layer worth attending: when a member takes the stage at an I Combinator event, they are there to teach something specific and useful — how they got their first ten customers, what a term sheet actually cost them, why a hire failed.

No product pitches. No recruitment drives dressed up as talks. The company name is a credential, not the content. That constraint is why the room fills.

Who this is for

The bar.

  • You are building in Ireland, or building from Ireland for elsewhere.
  • You are early in your career and already shipping — students, dropouts and first-time founders all qualify.
  • There is something live: users, revenue, a product in people's hands, or a company already trading.
  • You will give as much as you take. The circle only works if members answer each other's questions honestly.

Not a fit if you are looking for a place to sell to other founders, or if you want the network without contributing to it.
